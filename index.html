Search sites

Add websites you want to search for an anime. Each one gets a button on the anime's details window and on your list. Put {q} where the anime's title should go, e.g. https://website.com/catalog?search={q}. If you leave {q} out, the title is added to the end.

Anime info

Where scores, ranks, summaries and art come from. Your list always uses MAL IDs, so you can switch any time. If one source can't be reached, the other is used automatically.

Scores and ranks change over time. Refresh pulls the latest info and art for every anime on your list.
